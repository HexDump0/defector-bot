03Under the hood

The rules,
in plain English.

01

Lead with trust

Round one is always cooperate. No memory, no suspicion.

02

Read the opening

Collect five opponent moves. CDCCC means exploit; DDDDD means hard; every other opening means friend.

03

Forgive your own retaliation

In observe and friend modes, cooperate after the opponent cooperates—or after your own recent defection—to prevent an endless feud.

04

Remember repeated betrayal

From round 11 onward, a friend with at least two opponent defections in the last eight rounds becomes hard forever.
